\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting.}
3D Gaussian Splatting~\cite{kerbl2023gaussian} represents scenes as a set
of anisotropic Gaussians rasterized via differentiable tile-based
blending, achieving photo-realistic novel-view synthesis at real-time
rates. Extending 3DGS to dynamic scenes has proceeded along two axes.
\emph{Offline} methods fit a per-scene deformation field on top of a
canonical Gaussian set, either with neural
MLPs~\cite{luiten2024dynamic,duan2024gaussian, Yang2024Deformable3G} or
with explicit 4D primitives~\cite{wu2024gaussian}. These methods achieve
high fidelity but require hours of per-scene optimization and access to
the full sequence. \emph{Feed-forward} methods predict dynamic Gaussians
in a single network pass. \StreamSplat~\cite{wu2026streamsplat} is the
closest precursor: it processes uncalibrated monocular streams online
with a probabilistic position sampler, a bidirectional deformation field,
and an adaptive opacity fusion. Concurrent streaming feed-forward
work~\cite{song2026streaming} targets static scenes with a voxel cache.
All of these methods work in a normalized canonical space and do not
recover metric scale; \StereoSplat preserves the feed-forward, online
property while adding metric scale through a stereo front end.

\paragraph{Stereo Matching and Metric Depth.}
Learned stereo matching has converged on iterative cost-volume
architectures. RAFT-Stereo~\cite{lipson2021raft} introduced all-pairs
correlations with a ConvGRU updater; IGEV-Stereo~\cite{xu2023igev}
augments this with a geometry encoding volume that aggregates non-local
context, ranking first on KITTI at the time of publication with strong
cross-dataset generalization. We use IGEV-Stereo as our disparity
backbone, but any stereo matcher that outputs a per-pixel disparity and a
confidence map drops into the same slot. Monocular metric depth
estimators~\cite{yang2024depth} exist but rely on dataset-specific scale
recovery and do not generalize to arbitrary scenes; the stereo triangulation
formula $Z = b f / d$ is exact given calibrated intrinsics and is the
anchor we use.

\paragraph{Feed-Forward 3DGS.}
pixelSplat~\cite{charatan2024pixelsplat} regresses Gaussians from a
calibrated image pair; NoPoSplat~\cite{ye2024noposplat} removes the pose
requirement; DepthSplat~\cite{chen2024depthsplat} adds a cost-volume depth
prior. These methods target static scenes. \StreamSplat extends the
paradigm to dynamic, online streams but keeps the orthographic canonical
space. \StereoSplat is the first to bring the feed-forward dynamic
setting into metric scale via stereo.

\paragraph{Display Artifacts in Dynamic GS.}
Temporal flickering in static regions is a documented failure mode of
online dynamic GS and is addressed in~\cite{yun2025compensating} by
learning a residual error map. FAST-GS~\cite{zhang2026fastgs} replaces
polynomial motion with Fourier series to capture high-frequency motion
and reduce long-term drift; DG-4DGS~\cite{chen2026dg4dgs} adds a
deformation graph for cross-frame geometric alignment.
Fov-GS~\cite{fan2025fovgs} accelerates dynamic-scene rendering with
foveated splatting. Popping --- abrupt Gaussian appearance and
disappearance --- is noted in~\cite{wu2026streamsplat} and addressed there
with an adaptive opacity gate. Our streaming display module combines a
motion-adaptive EMA (targeting flicker) with an opacity-stability filter
(targeting popping), both training-free and orthogonal to the underlying
reconstruction.
